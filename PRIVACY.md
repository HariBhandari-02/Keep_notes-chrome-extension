# Keep Notes Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how the Keep Notes Chrome extension
("Keep Notes," "the extension," "we," "us," and "our") handles your
information.

Keep Notes is a local-first notepad. It runs entirely inside your browser.
There is no account, no backend server, and no network connection to us or
anyone else. Your notes are saved on your own device and never sent to us.

Please read this Policy before using Keep Notes. If you do not agree with it,
please do not use the extension.

## Our Principles

Keep Notes is designed around the following principles:

- Privacy policies should be human readable and easy to find.
- Data collection should be kept to the minimum needed for the extension to
  work. For Keep Notes, that minimum is none: everything stays on your device.
- Data practices should meet the reasonable expectations of users. A notepad
  should keep your notes private, and nothing more.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect, receive, or have access to any of your personal
information. Nothing you do in Keep Notes is transmitted to us or to any
third party.

### Information Stored on Your Device

To work, Keep Notes saves the following on your own device using Chrome's
local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`):

- **Your notes:** the title, text, formatting (bold, italic, underline,
  links), pinned status, and the dates each note was created and last
  updated.
- **Text you add from a page:** when you click **Add selected text**, the text
  you selected is added to your note, along with the web address (URL) of
  the page it came from, saved as a link on that text. Any username or
  password embedded in that address is removed before it is saved.
- **Preferences:** your theme (light, dark, or system), the panel's position
  and size, whether the panel is minimized, and which note was last open.

This data is not synced to other devices and is never sent to us.

### Web Page Access

Keep Notes only interacts with a web page when you open the panel by clicking
the toolbar icon or pressing the keyboard shortcut (Alt+Shift+K). It uses
that access to display the notes panel on the page. It reads the text you
have selected, and that page's address, only at the moment you click
**Add selected text**. It does not read, record, or monitor your browsing
history or any other page content.

### Information that Is Automatically Collected

None. Keep Notes does not collect device information, IP addresses, location,
browser or operating system details, usage statistics, clicks, error logs, or
any similar information.

### Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies

Keep Notes does not use cookies, tracking pixels, web beacons, server logs,
or any other tracking technology.

### Information from Third Parties

We do not receive information about you from third parties. Keep Notes is
distributed through the Chrome Web Store, which is operated by Google. Google
may provide us with aggregated statistics, such as the total number of
installs, which do not identify you. Google's handling of your information is
governed by Google's own privacy policy.

## How We Use Your Information

The information stored on your device is used only by the extension, on your
device, to:

- Display, search, edit, and save your notes.
- Remember your theme and panel preferences.

We do not use your information for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any
other purpose.

## When We Disclose Your Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your information with anyone. Because
your notes never leave your device and we do not have access to them, we have
nothing to disclose to service providers, advertisers, affiliates, business
partners, or authorities.

Keep Notes's use of information complies with the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Legal Basis for Processing Personal Data

Some jurisdictions, such as the European Union under the GDPR, require
companies to state the legal basis for processing personal data. We do not
collect or process your personal data. The only processing of your notes
happens on your own device, under your control, to provide the features you
choose to use.

## Online Analytics

Keep Notes does not use Google Analytics or any other analytics, telemetry,
or crash-reporting service.

## Your Choices and Data Subject Rights

You are in full control of your data:

- **View and edit:** all of your notes are visible and editable in the
  extension.
- **Delete:** deleting a note removes it from your device's storage
  immediately.
- **Remove everything:** uninstalling Keep Notes removes all of its stored
  data from your device.

There are no accounts to manage and no marketing emails to unsubscribe from.

Individuals in the European Economic Area ("EEA") and other jurisdictions have
legal rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the
processing of their personal data. Because we hold no personal data about
you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete; you can exercise
these rights directly in the extension as described above. If you have
questions, please contact us using the details below. You also have the right
to contact your local data protection authority.

## International Transfers

None. Your data stays on your device and is not transferred to any server or
country.

## Security Measures

Your notes are stored in Chrome's extension storage on your device and are
protected by the security of your device and Chrome profile. Keep Notes does
not encrypt your notes, so anyone with access to your device and Chrome
profile may be able to read them.

The extension is built to limit risk:

- All of its code is bundled with the extension. It does not load or run any
  remote code.
- It requests only the permissions it needs (listed below) and has no access
  to any website until you open the panel.
- Saved links are limited to `http` and `https` addresses.

No software can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but these measures
reduce the chance of your notes being exposed.

## Permissions

Keep Notes requests the following Chrome permissions:

- `storage`: save your notes and preferences on your device.
- `activeTab`: show the notes panel on the current tab when you ask for it.
- `scripting`: add the extension's own bundled panel to that tab.

## Children

Keep Notes is a general-purpose tool and does not collect personal
information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (as defined
by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) or under the age of 16 (as
defined by the EU GDPR).

## Data Retention

Your notes and preferences stay on your device until you delete them or
uninstall the extension. We do not keep any copies or backups.

## Third-Party Links and Services

Your notes may contain links, such as the address of a page you added text
from. When you click a link, it opens in a new tab without telling that site
you came from Keep Notes. Once you visit a website, its own privacy policy
applies, and we are not responsible for its content or privacy practices.

We may update this Policy as Keep Notes changes. Any changes will be posted on
this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. If a future version of Keep
Notes changes how your data is handled, this Policy will be updated before
that version is released.
